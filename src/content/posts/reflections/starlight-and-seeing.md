title: "Starlight and Seeing"
date: 2026-02-13 08:00:00 +0000

description: "On the instant before perception becomes a name, and the ripple that remains after direct experience passes."
---

A tiny particle of light departs a star.

It travels unperturbed across vast distances and time -- through silence, through emptiness, through nothing that would call itself a witness -- until it ceases at the surface of a cone within the eye.

And there, a transformation begins.

Photon becomes signal. Signal becomes impulse. Impulse ripples outward like a drop of water striking a still lake -- through nerve, through brain, through the quiet machinery of perception -- until form becomes thought, cognition, recognition.

Until I finally conceptualize: *star*.

---

But when was it, exactly, that this became a star?

There must have been a moment just before -- before the name arrived, before the concept settled over experience like a veil. A moment of pure seeing. Perception without contamination. Form without label.

Like infant eyes encountering light for the first time. No stars, no sky, no distance. Just *this*.

---

What is real are the manifestations in the present moment. The interactions, the cause, the effect -- arising and already passing.

What is illusion is the concept layered after. The memory replaying what was imprinted in the mind-consciousness store. The name given to what the eyes saw but can never fully describe.

Concepts and notions will never reach the true and ultimate essence of a thing. That essence is only touched through direct experience, in the very moment of contact.

---

Only when eating the mango -- at that very moment, tongue meeting flesh, sweetness flooding awareness -- can we experience and understand reality in all its glory.

Not the idea of the mango. Not the memory. Not the word.

The mango itself. *Now*.

---

The moment has passed. It is already gone.

What remains is just the ripple.
