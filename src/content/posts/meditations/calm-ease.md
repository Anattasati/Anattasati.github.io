title: "Calm and Ease"
date: 2025-07-13 07:30:00 +0000

description: "A simple breathing verse for returning to calm, ease, and the present moment."
---

> In, Out  
> Deep, Slow  
> Calm, Ease  
> Smile, Release  
> Present Moment  
> Wonderful Moment
> 
> Breathing in, I calm my body.  
> Breathing out, I smile.  
> Dwelling in the present moment,  
> I know this is a wonderful moment.

— Thich Nhat Hanh
